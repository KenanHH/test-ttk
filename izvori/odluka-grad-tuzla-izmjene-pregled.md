## Official prečišćeni tekst

None found. Čl. 13 of the 4/21 amendment ordered one within 30 days, and the council also ordered one (user says Jan 2022). I scanned every gazette issue I could obtain from 2021 to Oct 2026 (full list below) for "prečišćeni tekst Odluke o uvjetima i načinu pribavljanja i raspolaganja nekretninama" and for citations carrying "prečišćeni tekst". No such act was found. Every later citation of the Odluka lists the plain amendment chain without "prečišćeni tekst" (e.g. "5/19, 6/19, 4/21, 9/22, 2/23 i 9/23" in 2023-2025 issues, and "5/19, 6/19, 4/21, 9/22, 2/23, 9/23 i 3/26" in 2026 issues). The only "prečišćeni tekst" order in 1/22 relates to a different Odluka (no act title containing the real-estate Odluka). Absence is not proven for issues I could not download (below).

Issues scanned (text search): 2019: 5, 6; 2021: 4-13; 2022: 1-14 (4/22 file "4-22-1"); 2023: 1-3, 5-13 (2/23, 9/23 inspected); 2024: 2-15 plus two unlabelled "FINAL-STAMPA" files; 2025: Jan-May issues plus 6-11, 13-16; 2026: 1-8, 11-13. NOT obtained: 4/2023 text was reviewed earlier from the supplied raw file; 1/2024 (not in media library), 12/2025, 9/2026, 10/2026. A later amendment could theoretically sit in those; citations in 11/2026-13/2026 and 5/2026-6/2026 still end with "9/23 i 3/26", which argues against it.

## Caveats on the consolidation

- Punctuation of amended passages follows the amending acts' quoted text literally. Examples: čl. 3 tač. a) now ends with a full stop (the quoted text ends "određeno.") although b)-e) end with commas; čl. 27 st. (1) tač. e) now ends with a comma after "51 %," and the original full stop after "nadležne službe" is not kept; čl. 29 tač. f) (3/26) ends with a full stop followed by tač. g).
- Čl. 8 and čl. 38 had no "(1)" marker in the original; amendments add "(2)" to them.
- Čl. 27 retains stav (4) number; the 2/23 amendment does not renumber.
- Član 5 of the 4/21 act repeals čl. 11 without renumbering; čl. 8a was inserted after čl. 8. Article numbers in the file are the ones that apply after the amendments.
- Čl. 33 of the original has two paragraphs numbered (3) (printing error in the gazette); kept as printed.
- No UNVERIFIED markers were needed: all six amendments were located and applied.
